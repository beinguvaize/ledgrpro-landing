Yes. LedgrPro offers seamless Excel and CSV import protocols constructed specifically to map Tally exported ledgers, masters, and inventory lists effortlessly.

LedgrPro runs smoothly in modern web browsers. All processing is lightning-fast and cloud-backed, meaning you can access the storefront from anywhere with active internet.

Your data is stored with enterprise-level AES-256 encryption and backed up daily. You can configure granular role-based permission grids guaranteeing employees only see what's strictly necessary.
