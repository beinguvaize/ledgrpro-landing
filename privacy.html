Privacy Policy.

How we protect your business ledgers and customer metrics. Fully compliant with the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Last Modified: May 13, 2026 | Version 2.2

1. Data Hosting & In-Region Storage

All StockMate accounting ledgers, client records, and transactional databases are hosted securely on Supabase backend infrastructure. To ensure legal compliance and maximum loading speeds for the real India, our database instances are physically located in local availability regions (AWS Mumbai).

2. Absolute Zero-Selling Commitment

We maintain a strict stance on client confidentiality: we do not sell, lease, or monetize your accounting or customer data to third parties. Your client list and margin structures are yours alone.

3. Compliance with the IT Act, 2000

StockMate maintains technical and organizational safeguards mandated by the Information Technology Act 2000 (India) and subsequent amendments regarding sensitive personal data and information processing standards.

4. Financial & GST Data Retention

We retain historical ledger files while your subscription remains active. Should your account remain dormant, we follow structured regulatory guidelines to secure records while enabling you to access historical GSTR spreadsheets.

5. The Right to Deletion

You retain absolute ownership over your database console. You may trigger a request to delete your complete administrative console and tenant data at any time. Upon processing, our systems securely purge all tenant storage allocations.
