Privacy Policy.

How we secure and handle your transactional and organizational intelligence.

Last Modified: May 13, 2026 | Version 2.1

1. Overview

LedgrPro ("we", "our", or "us") provides a cloud-based multi-tenant ERP ecosystem. This policy details how we harvest, store, and secure personal identifiers and transactional metrics associated with your administrative account and end clients.

2. Data We Process

To power your business operations, we ingest standard structured attributes:

  • Account Credentials: Admin email, phone number, validated GSTIN registration credentials.
  • Operational Data: Vendor ledgers, price catalogs, client directories, and geo-route manifest logs.
  • Telemetry: IP hashes, diagnostic log streams, and interface clickstreams to optimize speed.

3. Data Locality & Residency

We adhere strictly to regional sovereignty statutes. For customers operating in India, transaction ledgers and physical file volumes are homed exclusively in local availability regions (AWS/Supabase Mumbai) to align with regulatory expectations.

4. Third-Party Processors

We only relay raw payloads to verified operational pipelines, specifically:

  • Fintech Gateways: Razorpay (for transactional reconciliation).
  • Government Gateways: National Informatics Centre (NIC) APIs for IRN routing.
  • Infrastructure: Supabase / PostgreSQL (Encrypted operational databases).

5. Contact & Exercising Rights

Questions or deletion inquiries? Contact our Data Protection Officer directly at privacy@ledgrpro.com.
